Omnidirectional tabletop proposal · 2026

A launcher with no head of the table.

One shared horizontal touch screen. Approach from any edge, recognize a large game icon, and tap once. The selected game owns everything that follows.

01 · The installed surface

A game ring anyone can reach.

Large semantic tiles face outward around one rotatable ring. Four equal edge handles reposition the current games; tapping the center logo advances through stable alphabetical pages of eight.

02 · The physical context

Designed for people around a table.

The product is not a phone or desktop site. Its acceptance context is a landscape display embedded flat in a table, with participants reaching from north, east, south, west, and corners.

  • At least 120 × 120 CSS-pixel game targets
  • Outward-facing title orientation
  • Standing, seated, glare, parallax, and multi-hand review

03 · Interaction contract

Touch means exactly one thing.

  1. Find: recognize a title/icon tile near your edge.
  2. Browse: drag the current ring or tap the center for the next eight games.
  3. Tap: stationary game press/release shows feedback and opens the URL once.
  4. Hand off: the launched game owns orientation, players, and setup.

Engineering decisions

A strict launcher boundary.

Device
Fixed 16:9 horizontal table display; no phone or desktop layout.
Authentication
Silent anonymous Firebase session; no account UI.
Database
Same read-only Applications records: Title, Icon, URL.
Interface
Semantic DOM/CSS radial ring with Pointer Events.
Testing
1920×1080 emulator E2E from N/E/S/W/corner; zero-pixel Linux baselines.
After tap
No launcher screen remains; the selected URL owns the experience.
